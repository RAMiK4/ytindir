## ✨ New Features

- YouTube downloads open straight on the new quick list, which fills in with the video's real details
- The quick download panel has an All formats button and a Download button to download the chosen quality right away
- A new setting for those who want a choice in the quick download panel to go back to the regular panel
- Audio formats show their bitrate next to the format; more details for the MP3 option
- Frosted glass look for the navigation bar, now more compact (Android 12 and up)
- The terminal is back in the More screen; Proxy and Force IPv4 are back in the settings

## 🔧 Changes

- The chosen quality, audio and container no longer reset
- Only one audio track per language can be picked in the all formats list
- Qualities below 480p are back in the quick list, for older low resolution videos
- Settings are reorganized
- More compact info dialogs
- English, Turkish and Russian translation fixes
- Unused languages and libraries removed from the app, settings that did nothing are hidden
- Bug fixes and other improvements
