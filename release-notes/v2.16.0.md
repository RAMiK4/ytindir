## ✨ New Features

- New YouTube downloads open straight on the quick download list, which fills in as the video's data arrives
- Download button in the quick download list: pick a quality or format and download it right away (or turn on "Choice opens the download card" in the settings to go back to the card instead)
- Audio formats show their bitrate as a badge next to the name; the MP3 option shows MP3 · 192 kbps with Opus → MP3 under it
- Frosted glass bottom navigation (Android 12 and up), more compact, with a softer look
- Certificate errors: the app now trusts the phone's own certificates too, and the error explains what happened with a "Skip certificate check, retry" option
- The terminal is back in the More screen; Proxy and Force IPv4 settings are visible again

## 🔧 Changes

- Your chosen quality, audio and container no longer reset when the video's data arrives
- Only one audio track per language can be picked in the all formats list
- Qualities below 480p are shown in the quick list again
- Settings: Backup and restore and About are sections of their own, Advanced is visible, SponsorBlock has its own section in Processing
- A more compact error dialog: the text scrolls, the actions are always visible
- New brand green, also on the home screen shortcut icons; the shortcuts open the right screens again
- Russian, Turkish and English translation fixes
- A smaller app: unused languages and libraries removed
- Bug fixes
- Other improvements
