### YTindir 2.13.1: H.264 for social videos, Instagram without a working login

## ✨ New features

- Convert social videos to H.264: Instagram, X, TikTok, Facebook and VK still download their best quality, and a VP9, AV1 or HEVC video is converted to H.264 on the device before it's saved, at a quality close to the original, so it plays in every player and app. Settings → Processing → "Convert social videos to H.264" (off by default; the conversion takes a little longer).

## 🐛 Bug fixes

- Instagram posts no longer fail with "Failed to parse JSON" when the account saved in Cookies has been logged out: the post is read as a logged-out visitor (the website version) instead, for both the download options and the download itself. Once you log in again in Cookies, downloads use the account again, including the Instagram app quality audio.
- "Compatible Video" re-encodes now keep a quality close to the original (CRF 18 instead of FFmpeg's default 23) and run faster (veryfast unless another preset is chosen); converted audio is AAC 192 kbps.

## 🔧 Other improvements

- New update cards for the app and yt-dlp: the status on top, the version on one line that no longer breaks apart, channel and last check under it, and a full-width "Check now" button.
- Settings → Language now opens the app's own language list instead of jumping to Android's settings; App info → Language keeps working too.
- Clearer "Compatible Video" description, and Turkish texts for the update cards.
