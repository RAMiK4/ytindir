### YTindir 2.13.0: Facebook and VK Video, better Instagram audio

## ✨ New features

- Facebook downloads now default to the best dash quality and are saved with short names: username_videoid, like Instagram, X and TikTok.
- VK Video downloads now default to the best hls or dash stream instead of the lower quality mp4 files; file names stay the video's own title.
- Better Instagram audio: with an Instagram account added in Cookies, video downloads get the higher quality audio the Instagram app plays instead of the website's lower bitrate audio, while the video stays the website's best H.264 or VP9 stream. If the app audio can't be fetched, the website audio is kept. Settings → Processing → "Instagram app quality".
- Compatible audio: xHE-AAC audio, which some players and apps can't play, is converted on the device to AAC-LC 192 kbps, loudness-matched to how Instagram plays it; the video itself isn't re-encoded. Settings → Processing → "Compatible audio (AAC)".

## 🐛 Bug fixes

- Quick downloads that start before the formats are loaded now also get short username_postid names on Instagram, X, TikTok and Facebook, instead of long tweet texts or "Video by …", which also added "(1)" to other videos of the same account. The same title is embedded in the file's metadata.
- Posts with several videos (carousels) are saved as username_postid_1, _2… in quick and normal downloads alike, instead of name (1), name (2).
- Quick downloads show their file name in the history and the finished notification instead of the link or an empty title.
- The cookies screen no longer leaves the app's colors half light after you log in and come back: the cookies browser now runs separately from the rest of the app. Sites you were logged in to there may ask you to log in once more; cookies already saved keep working.
- A download no longer fails with "No such file or directory … info.json" when cookies were changed or yt-dlp was updated just before it started.
- The working folder under Android/data is now "ytindir_cache" on every install; installs that had opened the folder settings before the rename kept using "ytdlnis_cache".

## 🔧 Other improvements

- Cookies now say "Generated by YTindir", the ones saved earlier too.
