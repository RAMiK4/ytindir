## ✨ New features

- Compatible audio: videos whose audio is xHE-AAC (which some players and apps can't play) are converted on the device to AAC-LC 192 kbps, loudness-matched to how Instagram plays them; the video itself isn't re-encoded. Can be turned off in Settings → Processing → "Compatible audio (AAC)".
- Instagram app quality: with an Instagram account added in Cookies, video downloads also get the audio the Instagram app plays (higher quality xHE-AAC, converted to AAC-LC) in place of the website's lower bitrate audio; the video stays the website's best H.264 or VP9 stream. If the app audio can't be fetched, the website audio is kept. Can be turned off in Settings → Processing.
- Facebook downloads now default to the best dash quality and are saved as username_videoid, like Instagram, X and TikTok.
- VK Video downloads now default to the best hls or dash stream instead of the lower quality mp4 files; file names stay the video's own title.

## 🐛 Bug fixes

- Quick downloads that start before the formats are loaded now get short username_postid names on Instagram, X, TikTok and Facebook too, instead of long tweet texts or "Video by …" (which also added "(1)" to every other video of the same account). Posts with several videos (carousels) are numbered _1, _2… in quick and normal downloads alike, instead of name (1), name (2)
- Quick downloads now embed the same username_postid title in the file's metadata instead of "Video by …".
- The working folder under Android/data is now really "ytindir_cache": installs that had opened the folder settings before the rename kept using "ytdlnis_cache".
- Quick downloads now show their file name in the history and the finished notification instead of an empty title.
- The cookies browser no longer leaves the app's colors half light after you log in and come back: it now runs separately from the rest of the app. (Sites you were logged in to there may ask you to log in once more; cookies already saved keep working.)
