## ✨ New Features

- Instagram stories and highlights can be downloaded in high quality (up to 1080p@60fps where available; requires Instagram account cookies)
- Instagram audio (mp3) downloads are converted from the Instagram app's higher-quality audio
- Older TikTok videos are downloaded in TikTok's best available quality instead of 576p with 32 kbps audio
- VP9, AV1 and HEVC videos from Instagram, TikTok and other social media can be converted to H.264 at near-original quality (Settings → Processing)
- Search results can be sorted by Relevance, Newest, Most viewed and Oldest, and now show view counts
- Error messages now include a short explanation of what the error means and what to do
- More compact update cards for the app and yt-dlp sections in Settings
- The app language can now be chosen inside the app, without going to Android settings

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Posts no longer fail with "Failed to parse JSON" when the Instagram account in cookies has been logged out; they are downloaded with the web version instead
- "Compatible Video" conversions now keep the quality and are faster
- Other improvements
