<b>YTindir</b> is a clean, modern audio and video downloader powered by yt-dlp. It is based on YTDLnis, with the advanced tools tucked away and a setup that keeps itself working when websites change.

<b>Features</b>

- Audio and video downloads from more than 1000 websites, with format and quality selection
- Trimming: download only part of a video
- Download straight from the share menu of any app
- Self-repair: yt-dlp updates itself and a failed download is retried once with the new version
- Synced lyrics from lrclib.net, embedded into the audio file
- In-app updates
- YouTube PO token support and custom yt-dlp sources
- Cookie support for content you open with your own account
- YTindir colours or Material You, light and dark themes, high contrast mode
- 12 languages

YTindir is meant only for content you have the right to download.
